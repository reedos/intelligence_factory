Method

How the numbers are made

The site is a scenario model of one campus. Four choices set up its hardware, and every hardware figure on every page follows from them through the code described below. Token estimates add their own workload and environment inputs on top of that hardware model, and the Evidence page always shows one stated reference scenario rather than following the campus you last set. This page names the constants, shows where each comes from, and says what the model leaves out. The names in code are the site's own, in src/model/engine.ts and its neighbors.

Personal educational project based on cited public sources. Not an official publication of my employer or the companies discussed. Models are schematic; estimates and assumptions are identified.
