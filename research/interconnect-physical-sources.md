# Watts to Tokens — Interconnect Physical Plant Fact Sheet

Sourced figures for the *physical* layer behind the network: connector and cartridge
counts inside an NVL72 rack, fiber counts and duct hardware between buildings, patch
panels and meet-me rooms, long-haul/DCI cable and DWDM line systems, and public figures
on fiber scale in real AI clusters. Compiled 2026-09-26. This sheet does not repeat
link rates, switch specs, or module power — those are in `research/interconnect-sources.md`.
Basis labels match `power-chain-sources.md`: **Published spec** (a vendor or standards
body states it), **Industry typical** (trade press or reference designs agree),
**Estimate** (derived or uncertain — no single authoritative number exists).
