# Generation model corrections, 9/29/26

Scope: engine.ts and sections.js; root owns cards/evidence and compute designer owns physical hardware.

- P0: Rubin inherited 18 switch chips and 18 logical links/GPU. Corrected to 4 chips/tray × 9 trays = 36 chips/rack, and 36 logical links/GPU. The static diagram samples relationships with a shared fabric boundary, avoiding an unverified physical ASIC port map. Sources: https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/?p=111036 ; https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/?p=113993 ; https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/nvlink/ . Acceptance: public census and SVG show correct counts; no 72-port/ASIC or 5,184-wire assertion for Rubin.
- P0: Rubin scale-out counted one aggregate 1.6T endpoint as one physical NIC/optic. NVIDIA's March article lists eight ConnectX-9 NICs and one BlueField-4 per four-GPU tray. Model now uses two 800G NIC ports/GPU and physical endpoints for switch capacity, tier choice, link/module/fiber counts and external-network power. The chosen Quantum-X800 InfiniBand topology remains an illustrative alternative, not a claim about a particular site's deployment or Spectrum-6. Existing nicGbps API stays aggregate; nicPortGbps and nicsPerGpu drive physical counting. Whole-rack NIC and NVSwitch kW remain unchanged. Acceptance: 144 GPU-side modules/rack and endpoint-budget tests, with conserved energy.
- P0: H100 falsely counted BlueField DPUs. Baseline DGX H100 has two front-end dual-port ConnectX-7 cards/server. Corrected classification and zero DPU census while preserving the prior modeled two active front-end links/server and their fiber allowance, now explicitly labeled. Source: https://docs.nvidia.com/dgx/dgxh100-user-guide/introduction-to-dgxh100.html . GB200 and GB300 DPU counts are separately consumed through dpusPerTray (2 and 1 respectively).
- P1: current NVIDIA specification pages publish Rubin 3 TB/s NVLink and 19.2 TB/s HBM bandwidth, differing from older technical-blog 3.6/22 figures. Engine now follows current product tables. Root records the source discrepancy in evidence. Current page: https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/nvlink/ .

Validation: 80 targeted tests across engine, fleet, plant, generation inventory and public census/diagram suites pass; TypeScript passes. Physical wiring exactness remains deliberately unclaimed. The legacy internal nvlinkPairs field remains a compatibility estimate and is suppressed for Rubin in the public census; root also suppresses its BOM row. This report does not certify live visual quality.

A subsequent physical-module audit corrected H100 host grouping: four twin-port OSFP modules serve eight back-end NIC ports/server. `gpuPortsPerModule=2` affects only physical module count; optical power remains a per-port allowance. GB200/GB300 retain four single-port compute modules/tray, supported by the [NVIDIA networking interface table](https://docs.nvidia.com/dgx/dgxgb200-user-guide/networking.html). Switch module counts already divide by their twin-port grouping. NET.modules therefore reports modeled physical modules, not port equivalents.
