- Merchant/alternative accelerator tier -- the site models only NVIDIA GB200/GB300/Rubin GPUs; no card acknowledges AMD's Instinct MI400 series (MI455X: 432GB HBM4, CDNA 5) as an alternative compute option shipping in the same GB300-class rack generation. COMMENT: did not independently re-verify MI455X specs this pass, but this matches publicly known AMD MI400-series positioning against GB300/Rubin-class racks; plausible and worth adding if the site wants to acknowledge non-NVIDIA compute.
- Merchant Ethernet/InfiniBand switch ASICs as an alternative to NVIDIA's own fabric -- Broadcom Tomahawk 6/Tomahawk Ultra, Marvell Teralynx 10, Cisco Silicon One G200 and Arista 7060X6/7800R4 all compete for the same leaf/spine role the data:hall:leaf and data:hall:spine cards describe, but only NVIDIA Spectrum-X/Quantum-X800 are named (even after the edits above). COMMENT: valid gap -- I confirmed above that even the corrected data:hall:leaf edit only names NVIDIA parts; a short 'also built on merchant silicon such as Broadcom Tomahawk or Marvell Teralynx' callout on that same card would round this out and is consistent with how real fabrics are actually built (not every AI campus uses NVIDIA's own switches).
- Dedicated storage/SSD tier -- data.js only glances at storage ('E1.S drives hold local data' on power:tray:nic; 'SSDs, fans, management' as a ledger label for the H100 rack). There's no card describing the NVMe SSDs that actually back GB200/GB300 racks for checkpointing, KV-cache offload and data-lake storage (e.g. Micron 9650 PCIe Gen6, Kioxia CM10, Samsung PM1753, Solidigm D5-P5336). Would fit as a new part in PARTS.tray or PARTS.hall. COMMENT: reasonable scope gap; did not fetch each SSD product page this pass, so treat the specific model numbers as unverified until checked, but the general gap (no dedicated storage card) is confirmed correct from reading data.js in full.
- AEC (active electrical copper cable) / optical-DSP retimer chips -- Credo, Marvell and Astera Labs make the retimer/AEC DSP chips used on the scale-out (Ethernet/InfiniBand) side of the fabric. data.js's rack:spine card is explicit that NVLink itself uses no retimers, but nothing in data.js names the retimer/AEC chips that do sit on the uplink side (data:hall:uplinks or data:tray:osfp would be the natural home). COMMENT: confirmed by reading data.js that data:hall:uplinks and data:tray:osfp currently name no retimer/DSP vendor at all -- gap is real; Credo/Marvell/Astera Labs are all genuine merchant suppliers in this space per general industry knowledge, not independently re-verified via fetch this pass.
- Grid-to-rack solid-state transformer at the substation level -- power:hall:sst models the 34.5kV-to-800VDC conversion inside the hall, but there's no campus-scene card for the medium-voltage-to-800/1500VDC SST that Hitachi Energy and Siemens/Reinhausen are building at the substation itself (announced Oct 2025/Aug 2026, ahead of Rubin Ultra). COMMENT: consistent with what I found fetching the Infineon/SolarEdge release, which describes a similar medium-voltage-to-DC SST -- confirms this is a real, active product category distinct from the in-hall SST already on the page; a campus-level card is a legitimate addition, though I did not fetch Hitachi Energy/Siemens sources directly.
- UALink as a named open alternative to NVLink -- PARTS_DATA.rack's 'optical' card already names Google's TPU optical circuit switch as 'the optical alternative' to NVLink, but there's no equivalent card for UALink (the open scale-up standard backed by AMD, Intel and Astera Labs, spec released but not yet shipping). COMMENT: confirmed by reading data.js that PARTS_DATA.rack (both nvl and non-nvl branches) has an 'optical' card for Google TPU pods but no UALink card -- gap is real and the proposed placement (next to 'optical') fits the existing pattern of that scene.
