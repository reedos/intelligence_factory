# DGX H100 server: documented layout and density (10/01/2026)

Reed, 10/01/2026, on level 5 with `accel=h100`: "The whole H100 server seems quite empty, is that correct?"
It was not correct. The old drawing put the GPU baseboard on the chassis floor and a short CPU board on a raised
shelf over the rear half, with nothing under it, and PCIe rose through open air between the two. NVIDIA's own
figures show the opposite stack: the **GPU tray on top**, the **motherboard tray below it**, the **six power
supplies under both at the rear**, and a **midplane behind the front fans** that joins the two trays. Every
section below was read from the page itself (raw HTML or PDF text, or the labeled figure); quotes are verbatim.

## Topology (UG figure "DGX H100/200 System Topology")

The legend names ConnectX-7, ConnectX-7 Network Module, NVMe, PCIe Switches, NVSwitch, PCIe, 100 GbE and CPU
communication. Read from the figure:

- Each GPU has one PCIe link, straight to one ConnectX-7 in a network module; each module's four ConnectX-7
  link up to one CPU. **No PCIe switch sits between a GPU and its NIC.** The ConnectX-7 ASIC can act as the
  switch: the ConnectX-7 InfiniBand datasheet lists "Standalone ConnectX-7 application-specific integrated
  circuit (ASIC), supporting PCIe switch capabilities".
- Three PCIe switches: one per CPU fanning out to four NVMe drives and one dual-port ConnectX-7, and one
  from CPU0 to the four NVSwitch chips (management).
- CPU0 also has the two M.2 drives; CPU1 has the 100 GbE NIC; the CPUs talk to each other directly.

So the old "PCIe switches: GPU to CPU and NIC" card was wrong for this system and is corrected.

## Layout as drawn (tray units, 10 cm; front +z)

Documented by the figures: the stack and its proportions (rear figure: GPU tray about 60% of the height,
motherboard tray about 24%, supplies about 13%); the front (four by three fan modules over a strip of eight drives
with the console board in the middle); the rear (four OSFP in the middle of the motherboard tray, riser slots
3/1 left and 4/2 right, USB/VGA, BMC, LAN, UID, power, BMC reset, code display, COM); inside the motherboard tray,
rear to front: risers and OSFP, CPUs side by side across the width with eight DIMMs either side of each, then the two
network modules, then the midplane connectors; inside the GPU tray, rear to front: NVSwitches, the 2 × 4 GPU grid,
then the midplane.

Representative (not dimensioned by NVIDIA): every depth along z, the midplane's openings and connector shapes,
the power distribution board and copper under the motherboard tray, the PSU depth, the fan housing depth, the HGX
board outline, where the bus converters and the PCIe switches sit, the DensiLink cable path, the riser card
outlines, heat-sink fin counts and the drawn sizes of every heat sink. The PCIe switches are placed where the
motherboard-tray figure shows two small heat sinks between the CPUs and the network modules, and one on the GPU
baseboard's front edge (the GPU-tray figure shows a small heat sink there); the figures do not label them.

## Not found

- PCIe switch part numbers and exact positions; NVSwitch, DIMM, NVMe and fan power. Heat-source watts on those are
  carried as assumptions in the scene, not on cards.
- Whether "_F"/"_R" in the fan names mean front/rear rotors in series. The front figure shows each module as a
  two-fan cartridge behind a hex grille, a handle between the two openings, so the drawing puts two rotors side by
  side; the card says only "2 per module".
- Number of Cedar modules is stated by NVIDIA as two ("Two networking modules are installed on interposer
  board"); NVIDIA's pages never use the name "Cedar", which comes from ServeTheHome.

## Design-rule pass (same day)

Reed's standing rule set (layout, place and route as a competent engineer would draw them; a published drawing
wins where one exists). NVIDIA's figures fix the stack, the midplane, the NVSwitch row at the back of the GPU tray
and the module/CPU/riser order in the motherboard tray; everything below is the "as drawn" part, now laid out by the
rules and checked in `src/scenes/dgx-h100-rules.test.ts` (and the board artwork by `tray-pcb.test.ts`).

| area | violation in the first rebuild | rule | fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| GPU baseboard front | GPU PCIe ran sideways under the row of 54→12 V bus converters to connectors not in line with its column | route away from switching power stages; connector beside the part that talks to it | a PCIe connector straight in front of each GPU column; one straight single-layer run, no via |
| GPU baseboard power | one long 54 V strap across the board to a row of six converters; 12 V to the rear modules down the NVLink gap channels | entry → conversion → point of load, wide and direct; one interface per channel | 54 V connectors in the outer strips and the center, two converters straight behind each, each feeding the regulator rows beside it; rear modules fed straight back along the same row line |
| Midplane | 54 V blades in front of the center fan openings; PCIe stiles also used near power | nothing in the inlet path; separate channels | blades on the outer and center stiles only, PCIe on the two stiles between them |
| Motherboard tray, GPU PCIe | midplane connectors not in line with the ConnectX-7 columns (a jog across the interposer) | signal-flow placement | connectors in line with each ConnectX-7 column, straight runs |
| CPU to network module | modules offset from the CPUs with a jog; CPUs had no regulators | source next to sink; regulators at the load | modules in line with the CPUs, straight PCIe; a regulator row on each CPU's front edge fed straight from its power connector |
| Motherboard power | fed back along the interposer's center channel shared with drive PCIe | separate channels | outer strip, across behind the module, straight back to the regulator row |
| NVMe | drive PCIe detoured along the board's outer edge and doubled back to the switch | short and direct | drive connectors in the center channel, straight to the two switches |
| Storage NICs | switch-to-riser PCIe ran under the inner DIMM bank | keep-outs | down the center channel between the banks, behind them to the riser |
| DensiLink | cables swept diagonally over the CPU heat sinks | cables away from hot parts, bundled | one flat bundle over the center in cage order, two ties on center posts |
| Symmetry | NVLink starts, GPU PCIe lanes and supply positions not mirror-symmetric | mirrored parts, mirrored routes | mirrored |
